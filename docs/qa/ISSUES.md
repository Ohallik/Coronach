Format and severity: [TEST_PLAN.md](../TEST_PLAN.md) section 2 and section 5. Evidence folders are under `Builds/quality/QA/`. "Fixed (improved)" means much better, and a person wouldn't report it now. "Not re-seen" means the last capture didn't reach it, so nobody has confirmed either way. "By design" means the behaviour is what the plan asks for, and it stays unless Nathan decides otherwise. For the look reviews, the fix commit is the batch that cleared the finding.
