Unity documents that controller query capsules can be smaller than their displayed bounds ([Unity 6.4 manual](https://docs.unity3d.com/6000.4/Documentation/Manual/class-CharacterController.html)); the recorded query behavior is consistent with that limitation, not a measurement of its internal shrink factor.
