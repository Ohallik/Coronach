# New tracks, second batch: eight places that share a track (melody seeds, November 14, 2026)

A music audit found tracks stretched over too many different places. This batch gives eight of those places their own melody seed. They were made with the `game-melody-seeds` skill and saved beside the first batch in `C:\Users\natem\Projects\GameMusic\coronach\`, in folders 07 to 14, one folder per cue. Every seed has an audition page (Play/Stop, the notes bar by bar, the Suno prompt with a Copy button) and an upload WAV (melody only, 44.1 kHz mono, peak -3 dBFS, one full statement). To use one: open the page and listen, then in Suno upload the WAV, switch Instrumental on and paste the prompt.

There is one seed per cue. Each is a different kind of tune: a riff, a long-note anthem, a bell figure, a creeping half-step line, a folk lament, a hammered chant, a fanfare and a running line. The skill compared each one with the fifteen first-batch seeds and with the rest of this batch, and found none that it resembles.

## The order to generate them in

Do the first batch first ([NEW_TRACKS.md](NEW_TRACKS.md)): two of these seeds are written to sit next to its tracks.

1. **Ground Fight.** It plays in every ordinary fight on foot, so it is heard more than anything else in this batch. It is worth the most takes.
2. **Creature Boss.** Six boss fights in chapters 1 to 4, the first bosses a player meets.
3. **Aurora Shear.**
4. **The Orrery.**
5. **Picket of Teeth.**
6. **Nacre Grave.** Do it after the first batch's Coronach and listen to the two together. They should sound like relatives, not like the same tune.
7. **Separation Works.**
8. **Broken Ring.** Do it after the first batch's The House That Can Leave. The launch cue ends on an E chord and this one starts in A, so the cut from the launch into the Ring should sound like the next step.

The two on foot, the Terminator and the Continuance, move to this one, and any ground zone that should change music when a fight wakes can name it too. The riff drops a fourth, pushes across beat three, and changes its tail on every return so the loop never sounds pasted together. It stays between C4 and F5, below the effects. The middle turns darker (E-flat, F, D), and the ending turns straight back into the start.

## 8. Creature Boss (the eighth seed: why this one)

A second boss theme, for the wild creatures of chapters 1 to 4: the Burrower, the Bellows Below, the Cantor, the Seedthane, the Floodcrown and the Rotbloom (`EnemyDef.bossMusicId`, empty today). Alien Boss Battle keeps the Muster's people and the Compact: Hessa Korl, Vessa Korl, Drask in both his fights, and the Debris Cutter. The eighth boss with no track of its own is Vessa Korl, chapter 8's elite.

Why this and not the other two choices:

- **Boss fights.** An empty `bossMusicId` falls back to Alien Boss Battle, so that one track plays for eleven boss fights. Seven of them come before chapter 5. Boss fights are where a repeated track is heard most clearly.
- **The credits** already use Title Theme on purpose (`docs/chapters/ch10-wiring.md` section 8, and the first batch's "Title Theme stays for the credits"), so the game ends on the tune it starts with.
- **Tallow Drift** shares Hub Town Groove with home, but it is one station visited in chapter 1.

- **creature-boss-a-v2, It Lives Here** (24.0 s). A running line in F-sharp Phrygian dominant, a 6/8 gallop at a felt 120. The hook rears up a fifth from G to D (both of the scale's odd notes) in two notes that cut across the 6/8, two against three, then falls through the scale's wide step (C-sharp, A-sharp, G). The runs between circle the home note. The middle gallops up to the peak over the flat-two chord, whose pull back home the whole piece leans on. The creatures are strained rather than evil, and several are released rather than killed, so the tune is wild rather than villainous.

## Before you open the pages

- The WAVs are the melody alone on the skill's plain synth, which is what the skill recommends uploading. They were made up front so you can go straight to Suno. Each page's Download WAV button gives a version with the chords.
- Seeds ending in `-v2` went through the skill's fix pass. In the Picket, the creeping bars got their own rhythm, because the review heard the whole seed as one repeated bar. In Separation Works, the last bar's chord changed so that its first note belongs to it. In Creature Boss, the hook bar's chord now starts on G and moves to F-sharp, so its first note, G, belongs to it. The first drafts are not in the folders.
- One review flag was kept on purpose: the Orrery's octave leap is flagged as wide, and it is the signature.
- Five of the eight are not in 4/4. Aurora Shear is in 12/8, the Orrery in 9/8 (in three-bar phrases), Separation Works in 7/8, Creature Boss in 6/8 and Nacre Grave in 3/4. Each prompt names its metre, but Suno may turn it into 4/4 anyway.
- The page shows tempo in quarter notes. For the three compound metres the prompt gives the tempo you feel instead: Aurora Shear's 114 is felt as 76, the Orrery's 132 as 88 and Creature Boss's 180 as 120. This is expected.
- The Picket should stay nearly melody-free. If Suno writes a big tune over it, try another take.
- Ground Fight is meant to sit under swords and skills. It is worth hearing under a real fight before choosing a take.
- Once the tracks exist, these need updating: `tracks.json`; the zones' `musicId` and `fightMusicId`; the six creatures' `bossMusicId`; the chapter briefs' Music sections; and the music list in the credits (`CreditsRoll.cs`). With both batches in, Endless Flight would play nowhere, because Nacre Grave and the Returning Passage both move off it.
