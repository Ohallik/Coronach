# The fourteen seeds, made again as long seeds (November 27, 2026)

The first seeds ([NEW_TRACKS.md](NEW_TRACKS.md), [NEW_TRACKS_2.md](NEW_TRACKS_2.md)) came back from Suno without a strong enough hook, and as tracks that repeated one idea. All fourteen cues have been written again with the `game-melody-seeds` skill, this time as **long seeds**: 47 to 55 seconds each, built as a small song (a hook, the hook answered, a second tune of its own, a short bridge, the hook again), so Suno has more than one idea to build a track from. Each hook is one bold gesture you can hum: a leap, a lurching rhythm, an octave bell, a ta-da.

They are saved beside the old ones in `C:\Users\natem\Projects\GameMusic\coronach\`, in the same fourteen folders, with names ending `-long-a`. The old seeds are still there. For each cue there is an audition page (Play/Stop, the notes bar by bar, the Suno prompt with a Copy button) and an upload WAV (the melody alone, 44.1 kHz mono, peak -3 dBFS, one full statement, under a minute). To use one: open the page and listen; then in Suno upload the WAV, switch Instrumental on and paste the prompt.

## What changed from the first batch

- **Longer, with a second tune.** Every seed has a contrasting middle and most have a bridge. No seed's opening bar fills more than a third of its bars.
- **Bolder hooks.** The first batch mostly crept in on a pickup and moved by step from the second note of the scale. These start on the home note or the fifth and leap.
- **One seed for each cue**, not two or three to choose from. If a hook doesn't land for you, say which and I'll write that cue a different kind of tune.
- **The family is kept.** Five cues are still one melody family built on the Coronach: the launch, the final flight and the epilogue play its tune, the Broken Ring's fanfare turns out to contain it, and Nacre Grave is its sad relative. The Coronach itself is new, so all of them are.

## The order to generate them in

1. **Coronach.** Everything in the family follows from it. If you don't like it, stop and tell me before doing the next four.
2. **Many Hands** and **Ground Fight.** They are heard most, so they are worth the most takes.
3. **The House That Can Leave**, then **Broken Ring**: the launch ends on an E chord and the Ring starts in A, so the cut from one into the other should sound like the next step.
4. **Room To Turn Back**, **Neighbourhood Afloat**, **Nacre Grave**: check each against your Coronach; they should sound like relatives.
5. The rest in any order.

## Before you open the pages

- Nobody has heard these: I can check a seed's shape and its review, not whether the hook lands. Your ear is the test, and a cue you don't like gets a different kind of tune, not a tweak.
- The skill's review passed every seed for length, variety and singable leaps, with three things kept on purpose. The Orrery's octave leap is flagged as wide, and it is the signature. The family seeds are flagged as sounding like the Coronach, which is the point. And the Picket's half steps are deliberate.
- Six are not in 4/4: Neighbourhood Afloat and Aurora Shear in 6/8, the Orrery and Nacre Grave in 3/4, Separation Works in 7/8, Creature Boss in 12/8. Each prompt names its metre, but Suno may square it off.
- The WAVs are the melody alone, which is what the skill recommends uploading. Each page's Download WAV button gives a version with the chords.
- A long seed is under a minute so that it fits Suno's upload limit for every plan.
- Once the tracks exist, the same things need updating as before: `tracks.json`, the zones' `musicId` and `fightMusicId`, the six creatures' `bossMusicId`, the chapter briefs' Music sections and the credits' track list (`CreditsRoll.cs`).
