# New tracks: the last six cues (melody seeds, November 11, 2026)

Six places in the finished story have no track. Each one now has melody seeds, made with the `game-melody-seeds` skill and saved in `C:\Users\natem\Projects\GameMusic\coronach\`, one folder per cue. Every seed has an audition page (Play/Stop, the notes bar by bar, the Suno prompt with a Copy button) and an upload WAV (melody only, 44.1 kHz mono, peak -3 dBFS, one full statement). To use one: open the page and pick, then in Suno upload the WAV, switch Instrumental on and paste the prompt.

Five of the cues are one melody family built on the Coronach tune. Everything derived (cues 2, 5 and 6, the opening of Last Mooring, and the second strain of each Many Hands seed) comes from **coronach-walk-a**. If you pick a different Coronach, say which one and those get re-derived from it.

## The order to generate them in

1. **Coronach.** Everything else follows from the one you pick.
2. **Last Mooring.** Its held D is what Room To Turn Back lets go, so make sure Suno kept the held opening before you do cue 5.
3. **The House That Can Leave.**
4. **Room To Turn Back.**
5. **Neighbourhood Afloat.** Check it against the Coronach you generated: it should be the same tune with new chords.
6. **Many Hands.** It is its own tune and is heard most, so it is worth the most takes.

## Before you open the pages

- The WAVs are the melody alone on the skill's plain synth, which is what the skill recommends uploading. Normally it renders a WAV only after you pick; these were made up front so you can go straight to Suno, and the ones you don't use can be ignored. Each page's Download WAV button gives a version with the chords instead.
- Seeds ending in `-v2` went through the skill's fix pass after its review flagged them. The flagged drafts are not in the folders.
- Some review flags were kept on purpose. last-mooring-c's octave leap is its signature. last-mooring-a-v2 counts as repeating recent habits, but its pickup into a held D and its close on D are the point. And the derived seeds "sound like" coronach-walk-a, which is the family resemblance the brief asked for.
- "Two short, one long", the Coronach's pickup, is the lantern signal in the scripts (Maru's lamps in the Coronach, Pell's count in the passage).
- Once the tracks exist, `tracks.json`, the atlas's music map and the chapter briefs' Music sections will need updating. Today they still list existing tracks for these places.
