## Boss arena contract: the gantry (`MeretGantry`, phase two)

*What the fight needs of the room (stream H, November 14). The fight is built and tested on a plain floor in the ground yard (`LastMooringBossTests`, Meret region): her Kit is `S/Combat/BossController.MeretGantry.cs`, her couplings `S/Combat/MeretCoupling.cs`, her arms `S/Combat/MeretFrame.cs`. **How the fight uses the room.** Meret in her heavy frame (`MeretGantry`, 3.2 m, rigged, 1.3 m round) walks the gantry's deck at 2.2 m/s with a welding arm on her right and a press on her left. The press slams down a marked strip at the lead; the weld runs down a marked lane from her right side through the lead and burns behind it; the gantry's coolant sluices flood marked strips of the deck through the lead's and either side, leaving dry strips between (from her first coupling open, a second flood takes the dry strips); with an arm gone she charges down a marked lane; anyone at her feet is stamped off. An arm left overreached (a slam on nobody, a weld at full stretch) offers its coupling: a seat lights on the deck beside her shoulder, and a hero there takes its weight and eases it (Oriel's release). Her bar is held at each third until a coupling opens. At nought she takes the lift down to the well.
