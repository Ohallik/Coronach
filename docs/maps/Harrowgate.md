## What the place is for

The Vaskar Muster's capital: basalt terraces stepped up from a war harbour, where four thousand can stand on the muster field and every company has come home for thirty years. It is a town and not a barracks: families on the lower terrace with washing on lines and cook-smoke from every chimney, children who play "parley" on the family stair with washing pegs, armourers along a street where half the forges are cold because the smiths were sent to Scoria, a trophy gallery of things taken from other worlds under cards that say "yielded". The party walks in openly at the harbour gate under a parley flag that Hessa vouches for out of spite, is shown the town by a cadet nobody told not to, eats nothing in a kitchen that doesn't exist, is received by the Warmaster in his own oath hall, and answers him on the muster field with the whole town watching. Then the town answers him itself.

Users: the garrison (pikeguards in the Muster's gunmetal frames, sergeants, the guard wing of the third wing: Arrek and Sool), cadets at drill, families (mothers on the sash rota, children, old people on benches), armourers and dock hands, the Unsworn hidden behind Odrun's kitchen; and the named: Hessa Korl (gate-sergeant), Vessa Korl (passing through, once), Pip, Mother Odrun, Tovra (once she comes in the back way), Brekk, Warmaster Drask Vaal.

## Rules of construction

- **North is the back of every view, and the town climbs north.** The fixed camera (pitch 40, yaw 20, distance 19.5, fov 30) stands south-south-west of the party and sees about 5.5 m south and 14 m north of it at the hero's level, and much further where the ground falls away (about 29 m at the harbour's water from the wall). So the town is four levels stepped up toward the north: the **quay** (y 0), the **lower terrace** (y 2.4), the **upper terrace** (y 4.8) and the **oath hall's podium** (y 7.2). Every riser faces the camera and stands behind whoever walks below it; nothing on the camera's side of a walk is higher than the walk.
- **Every front faces south.** A building, a forge, a trophy case or a cell door that faces east or west is seen edge on by this camera, so each stands along the north side of the ground in front of it with its back to the next riser: houses and the kitchen against the lower riser, forges along the armourers' street's north side, the galleries' cases along their back walls, the oath hall at the head of the field, the cells cut into a south face.
- **Nothing tall stands between the camera and a walk, a talk or the field.** The gatehouse towers are the one tall thing south of a walked place (the gate's landing on the lower terrace); they fade (`SceneryOcclusion`) and the lower terrace's walk keeps 8 m north of them. The muster field's south side is the drop to the lower terrace with a knee-high kerb, so the camera's side of the duel is open air.
- **The edges are drawn.** South: the quay's kerb and the sea below it. East: the harbour, below the harbour wall's parapet. West and north: basalt crag rising out of the terraces (the oath hall backs onto it). Every rim a body could walk off has a kerb or a parapet on a `Block`; no `Boundary` stands in the open.
- **Townspeople live here, in the bodies of the people who live here.** Vaskar civilians in four coats (Brekk's, and the soot-dark, crimson-tabarded and floured greys of Tovra's, Drask's and Odrun's grades of it), told apart by what they carry, wear and do; the garrison in the Muster's frames (the pikeguard's gunmetal, the scavenger's and the sergeant's kits); cadets and children in Pip's grade at three-quarter and two-thirds size. Never two alike side by side; most have somewhere to be (`WalkLoop`). A townsperson in a named person's coat stands well away from that person.
- **Places are named constants** in `HarrowgateBuilder.Town` (`Arrival`, `HessaGate`, `F`, `OdrunAt`, ...): the ways, the scatter's keep-off, the bake's must-reach list, LookDev's views, this page, the tests and a route generator all read the same numbers. `Ways` are declared and `NothingStandsOnTheWays` (Rootmarket's check) refuses the built town if anything solid the builder placed, by hand or by helper, stands within .9 m of a way's line, a townsperson within 2 m, or a walker's loop within 1.6 m.

## The end, and where it is heard

The chapter ends in the harbour cells: `HessaArmouryGate` at the gate, `IdraLockCounted` on the stair, `DraskCustody` at his cell, then the distress band on every set in the harbour and Meret's all-stations, and Taren's "Nine hundred and four." This is the last place of the act, and the song the last two chapters are built on is first heard after it. So the landing is built to be stood on: a stone landing at the water under the bastion, the harbour's lamps coming on along the moles, the Long Patience dark at her berth, Auntie's jib over the water, a radio set on a cask by the cell door with its lamp lit, Odrun's tray on the bench outside Drask's cell, and nothing in the frame that is busy. From `vask.deposed` the armourers' forges are lit again and the hammers go (Pip: "They used to sing at the hammers. They've stopped."): the town's work starting up again above the landing is the last thing the street does in the chapter.
