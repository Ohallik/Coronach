# Chapter 8 - Harrowgate (the Vaskar Reach)

Brief for every stream working on chapter 8. Plan: [SHIPPING_PLAN.md](../SHIPPING_PLAN.md). Story bible: [STORY_CAMPAIGN.md](../STORY_CAMPAIGN.md), especially "A third answer: the Muster". ## What the chapter is for

The Vaskar **Muster** reads the Severance as the war of the age: worlds cut off from the departing roads will be defenceless, and whoever holds a caged passage can reach them, and rule them, for generations. Its foundry on the volcanic world Scoria is building **Bridles** from Oriel's plans, and it already has a young Choir in one. Warmaster **Drask Vaal** intends Sorrel's nursery next.

The party crosses the Muster's border, finds the conscripted smiths who hate what they are made to build, frees the caged juvenile with Oriel's release, and goes to the capital. The Vaskar are a people, not a personality: Harrowgate has children, cooks, gossip and dissent, and it is the **Unsworn** (deserters, conscripted smiths and the families who hide them) who depose their Warmaster. The heroes win a duel; the town wins the war.

- **What the party changes:** the Compact route lock on the Picket approach (the one Taren installed, years ago, as a job) is taken apart under Idra's direction. The Slag Colossus is destroyed and the juvenile freed. Drask is beaten in a duel, deposed by his own people and arrested, not executed.
- **What it gives the convoy:** the Unsworn smiths, who will forge the finale's release gear from Bridle metal, led by **Tovra Kesh**. Proof, in front of witnesses, that the release works on a living Choir.
- **Act turn:** Drask's standing order survives him. A Bridle is sprung on the **Nacre** route. The thrashing Choir wrecks the Nacre transfer berth, the single door through which the Compact was sending nine hundred households at a time. Meret concludes that moving roads cannot be made safe fast enough and **launches Stillwater early**.
- **Taren, Sela, Idra:** Taren admits the lock is his and that he knew what it was for when he took the job; Idra doesn't forgive him on the spot and spends the next operation giving him orders, which he follows. Sela flies the border dark and quiet and hates how good the Muster's charts are. Hessa Korl, last seen yielding in the Sunken Relay, is here, free again, and has to choose.

Sync in: about 22. Sync out: about 25. Play time: 80-100 minutes.

## Places

### PicketOfTeeth (space, combat) - rule: staged detection
The Muster's border: flak-curtain gates, sensor buoys, carrier patrol loops, tumbling asteroids that cast sensor shadows. Detection rises in three visible stages (**warning** buoys pulse amber; a **hunter wing** launches; the **carrier** turns), so the party can go quietly from shadow to shadow or fight through. At the approach stands the **route lock**: a Compact barrier across the one clean lane, installed years ago to keep "unregistered traffic" (Veyr's convoys, among others) out. Taren knows every bolt. Idra directs its removal, piece by piece, under fire.

### ScoriaFlows (wild, combat) - rule: the crust tells you
Lava deltas that crust over and break on a cycle: **dark crust is safe, glowing seams are about to go, bright flow kills** (heavy damage and a shove back to safe ground, never an instant death). Obsidian shelves, ash squalls that close visibility for a few seconds, Muster garrisons at the crossings, and the camps of the conscripted foundry clans. The **Unsworn camp** is the safe pocket: Tovra, a bench, a stall, Brekk.

### BridleFoundry (dungeon)
A magma-powered forge on three levels: the charging floor, the casting gallery where cage ribs are poured, the **heart** where a juvenile Choir hangs in a finished Bridle. Coolant sluices are the dungeon's switches: flooding a channel cools a pour and opens a way across; draining it re-arms a press. Vessa Korl holds the casting gallery. The Slag Colossus guards the heart.

### Harrowgate (town, safe, then the duel)
Basalt terraces around a war harbour: muster fields, the oath hall, armourers' streets, family quarters with washing lines and cook-smoke, trophy galleries of conquered worlds, and the hidden Unsworn quarter behind Mother Odrun's kitchen. People live ordinary lives inside an institution of war. The party walks in openly under a parley flag that Hessa, of all people, vouches for. The **muster field** is the duel's first arena; the **war harbour** from above is its second.

**Vessa Korl (elite, the casting gallery).** A thaw-lance and a squad of pikeguards. She retreats to the heart at half Integrity.

**Slag Colossus (boss, the heart).** A walking crucible war-engine. Its armour glows too hot to hurt: **cool it** by opening the coolant sluices around the arena (flag switches under fire), which darkens a plate for a few seconds (the opening); **break the two pour-arms**; then fight it on the cooling slag floor as it comes apart. Attacks: a pour that floods a marked sector, a stomp ring, slag bombs in a fan, a furnace breath down a marked lane. Destroyed. Then the release, performed on the living juvenile with Tovra's smiths holding the clamps: it goes, and everyone in the room watches it go.

**Warmaster Drask Vaal (boss, Harrowgate).** *Part one, the muster field, on foot:* a duel against a war-frame lance. Long thrusts down marked lines, a sweeping haft, a shield rush, and **command shouts** that summon a guard wing of two pikeguards (the Unsworn in the crowd begin pulling them away as the fight goes on: fewer answer each shout). Human-scale, legible, hard. **Checkpoint.** *Part two, in flight over the war harbour:* he takes his war-skiff and runs for the carrier; a pursuit through harbour cranes and flak, with his lance runs and the harbour's guns marked on the water. Beaten, he is brought down on his own muster field, where the Unsworn and half the garrison are standing. Odrun speaks. He is arrested.

## Story beats (in order)

1. **Many Hands.** Brekk's word from Scoria. Idra lays out the Picket. Taren says the lock is his.
2. **The Picket.** Quiet or loud. The lock, taken apart to Idra's count.
3. **Scoria.** The crust. Garrisons. Brekk brings them to the Unsworn camp. Tovra: "You want to break the cage. Good. I built it. I know where it's weak, because I put the weakness in."
4. **The Foundry.** Sluices. Vessa. The heart.
5. **The Colossus.** Destroyed. The release. The juvenile gone. Tovra sits down on an anvil and doesn't say anything.
6. **To Harrowgate.** Hessa at the harbour gate, demoted, unimpressed. The parley flag.
7. **The town.** Pip's tour. The trophy gallery (Sela finds Lanternwick's relay glass in it). Mother Odrun's kitchen.
8. **Drask.** He receives them courteously and explains himself. The duel is his idea: it is how the Muster settles a question.
9. **The duel and the pursuit.** The crowd. The shouts answered by fewer each time. The harbour.
10. **Deposed.** Odrun. Hessa opens the armoury gate for the Unsworn and will deny it. Drask, in custody: "You've won a field. I gave an order before I walked onto it."
11. **Act turn.** The radio. Nacre. A Bridle sprung on the Nacre route; the berth. Oda's voice among the distress calls. Then Meret, to all stations: Stillwater proceeds now. (Chapter 9.)
