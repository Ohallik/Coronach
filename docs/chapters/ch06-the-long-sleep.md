# Chapter 6 - The Long Sleep (Hoarfell)

Brief for every stream working on chapter 6. Plan: [SHIPPING_PLAN.md](../SHIPPING_PLAN.md). Story bible: [STORY_CAMPAIGN.md](../STORY_CAMPAIGN.md). How-to: [CHAPTER_GUIDE.md](../CHAPTER_GUIDE.md). Chapters 3-5 come first in the story. This chapter opens the third movement and brings the third playable hero.

## What the chapter is for

Meret has named the price of her plan: Sorrel's nursery. The convoy's answer has to move the young Choir, and the only way out for them crosses a cold passage. Nobody aboard knows how a juvenile Choir survives cold. Old charts name the **Lastlight herders** of the ice world Hoarfell, who live inside the ribcage of an adult Choir that froze there in the last Severance and who have kept its sleep for longer than they have kept records.

The party arrives to find the Muster already digging. Beneath the glacier stands an Ancelane army of Bridle-guard constructs, frozen where it marched, and the Muster's thaw-rigs are waking it rank by rank to serve the Warmaster's cages. A Rauk rescue officer, **Idra Sorn**, has been tracking the raiders from the Veyr frontier and has a plan to collapse the dig. It is her plan and her operation; the party follows it, and she joins them.

- **What the party changes:** the thaw rig is collapsed, the Host Marshal is put back to sleep (not destroyed: it is a machine doing its last order, and the herders want their dead left in peace), the Muster's crew is driven off the glacier.
- **What it gives the convoy:** the herders' knowledge of the long sleep, carried by herders who choose to travel; Old Tern stays. Idra's rescue cutters bring engines and crews: the convoy can now lift more than it carries.
- **What it sets up:** the Muster seen in force for the first time, and what it wants (Bridles, private war-roads, and Sorrel's nursery for itself); Ancelane Bridle-guards (chapter 7's Oriel explains them); Idra's knowledge of the Picket and of the Compact lock on its approach, which Taren installed (chapter 8).
- **Taren and Sela:** Taren follows somebody else's plan and finds it a relief. Sela, who was half persuaded at dinner, hears Old Tern describe a road that was held still and what it became.

Sync in: about 16. Sync out: about 19. Play time: 60-75 minutes.

## Idra Sorn - the third hero

**The Rauk** are the mountain people of Veyr: broad, heavy-shouldered, strong-jawed, a head shorter than a tall Vael and half again as wide. Slate-grey skin from charcoal to ash, fine-grained like stone, with thin **heat-seams** (ember-orange lines at brow, cheekbones, throat and knuckles) that brighten with effort or feeling. No hair: a low crest of blunt overlapping **horn-plates** sweeps back from the brow over the skull, and the brow plates lift and flatten with expression. Small low ears, deep-set eyes with pale silver irises. They have held the frontier against Muster raids for generations, which is why their towns drill and why their rescue crews fight.

**Idra** (F, 30s) is a rescue-cutter officer of the Veyr frontier guard: a burn scar across the left brow plate, rescue-orange collar, a coil of line she is never without. Excellent at rescue under fire; terrible at accepting help without counting it as a debt. Dry, exact, impatient with speeches. She gives orders in the fewest words that will work and thanks people as if it cost her. She does not instantly forgive Taren for the route lock (chapter 8). Her humour is deadpan understatement.

**Forms.** The Rauk don't shape Lattice. Idra wears a **cutter frame**: a rescue rig that unfolds around her.
- *Natural:* off-duty rescue fatigues in grey and orange, the frame folded as a pack and collar.
- *Braced (her ground form):* hazard-orange and steel plates, a tower **brace** (shield arm) on the left, a hydraulic **breaching hammer** on the right, a visor up on the brow so the face shows.
- *Flight:* a broad, blunt rescue cutter with hazard stripes, a tow winch at the stern and cutting arms folded along the hull.

**How she plays.** The guard hero, as Taren is the edge and Sela the emitter.
- Attack: a slow three-hit hammer chain; each hit lands harder and breaks more than Taren's, and the third knocks light enemies down.
- Guard (**Brace**): stops 85% instead of 65% and costs less Thrust; a Flash Guard releases a shockwave counter. Her dodge is a short hop with a brief invulnerable window.
- Skills: **Breach** (a shield charge down a line, Kinetic), **Tow Line** (harpoon: pulls a light enemy to her, or her to a heavy one, Pulse), **Shelter** (plants the brace: a dome that stops shots for both heroes for a few seconds), **Slag Hammer** (an overhead Plasma slam, heavy break), and learned later **Rescue Flare** (heals the partner and can raise a downed one at range), **Counterweight** (her next three guarded hits each answer with a shockwave), **Avalanche** (a leaping Kinetic slam in three beats), **Hold the Line** (enemies turn to her; her plating rises).
- EXTRA: **Last One Out** (a breaching charge, a shockwave, and half Integrity back to the partner, raised if down).
- In flight: the brace is a ram-prow guard (the only flight guard in the game), the hammer a short-range breaching cutter, Tow Line drags enemies and wreckage.

**Party of three.** Two are deployed and one rests; the deployed pair is chosen on the Party page in any safe zone or at a bench. Story scenes always have all three present. She joins at about the party's Sync with five skills known and levels like the others.

Portraits: two sixteen-cell sheets (Natural, and Braced with the visor up). Bodies: three (natural, braced, cutter).

## The people of Lastlight

Herders of rime-beasts: a furred, stocky, small-eared people in layered hide and carved bone, soft-spoken, with a herder's patience and a dry way with outsiders. They call themselves **the Kept** (kept by the Choir whose ribs roof their town). Expression instrument: long cheek-whiskers and a thick brow ruff.

| Name | Who | Voice |
|---|---|---|
| **Old Tern** | Herd-reader (F, very old). Knows the long sleep. Will not leave the frozen Choir her village grew inside. | Unhurried, exact, teasing. Tells things once. "You came to ask how the young ones sleep. Sit down. It's a long answer and you're letting the cold in." |
| **Hask** | Lead herder (M, 40s). Will travel if Tern says the knowledge should. | Practical, plain, counts beasts under his breath |
| **Iru** | Lamp-shrine keeper (F, young). Wants to go; afraid to say so in front of Tern. | Quick, shy, then not shy |
| **Vessa Korl** | A Muster dig-master (F), Hessa Korl's elder sister; commands the thaw operation; escapes at the end. Seen again at Harrowgate. | Cold where Hessa is proud. Thinks in tonnage. |

## Places

### AuroraShear (space, combat) - rule: current-riding
Hoarfell's magnetic storms draw visible aurora currents through a shattered ice ring. Flying **with** a current gives speed and refills Thrust; flying **against** one is slow and drains it. The lane is a sequence of currents to hop between, with ice shards as cover and Muster tenders and skiffs hauling thaw-rig parts along the fast lanes. Idra is first met here as a voice and a hazard-striped cutter that pulls the party out of a bad current.

### Lastlight (town, safe)
Built inside the ribcage of a frozen adult Choir: the ribs arch overhead at the back and edges, vents beneath keep the rib-halls warm. Longhouses, the vent bath-house, the carving yard, beast pens, the lamp shrine at the skull end. Warm ground and cold ground read differently (steam, bare stone, lamplight against blue snow shadow).

### FrozenHost (wild approach, then dungeon)
**The White Reach** approach: crevasse bridges, wind-carved seracs, a migrating rime-beast herd to pass through without stampeding it, whiteouts telegraphed by the wind (visibility closes, vent pockets are shelter). Then the **dig**: three levels down through blue ice in which the Ancelane host stands frozen in ranks. Thaw-rigs wake a rank at a time as the party descends: the thaw is the dungeon's clock and its light (blue ice gone amber). Idra's plan: break the three **couplings** that feed the thaw rig, bottom to top, so the collapse runs upward behind the party. The **Host Marshal** wakes at the bottom.

**Host Marshal (boss, the lowest gallery).** An Ancelane construct commander, twice a guard's height, with a standard-spear and a cage-shield. It rallies thawed soldiers from the walls (the ranks step out of the ice as the rig runs). Phase one: spear lunges down marked lines, a shield bash, a rallying call that wakes three guards. The three thaw **couplings** around the gallery are break targets: each one broken refreezes a third of the wall, and the ranks there stop. Phase two, last coupling gone: the Marshal fights inside a closing ring of ice with its own heat failing, slower but hitting harder, its sweeps cracking the floor. At zero it kneels and the frost takes it: returned to sleep.

## Story beats (in order)

1. **Many Hands.** The chart. Sela names the herders. Ilo's footnotes get their first new entry: Lastlight.
2. **The Aurora Shear.** Currents. Muster tenders. A hazard-striped cutter and a voice: "Unmarked craft. You're in the slow lane and it's about to be the wrong lane. Follow me or don't."
3. **Lastlight.** The ribs. Old Tern doesn't get up. Why the Muster is digging; what the host is. Idra, already here, already arguing with Hask about evacuating the pens.
4. **Idra's plan.** Said in five lines over a carved map: couplings, bottom to top. Taren asks one question. She answers it. "Good. You'll do."
5. **The White Reach.** The herd. The first whiteout.
6. **The dig.** Vessa Korl on the rig's loudhailer. The ranks waking. Idra playable from the first coupling's gallery (she joins the party here).
7. **The Host Marshal.** Returned to sleep. Vessa's lifter leaves with what it has: a crate of Bridle plans and a dozen waking guards.
8. **Old Tern.** How the young ones sleep (cold slowly, never alone, sung to: the herders hum to their beasts and the Choir answers to pressure and rhythm). What happened to this one: it was held. The old rulers wanted a road that stayed. She stays; Hask, Iru and the herders go.
9. **Chapter end.** Idra's cutters dock at Many Hands. She reads the convoy's lift figures and is rude about them, then fixes them. The Bridle plans Vessa took were Ancelane: Ishen, sent for from Lanternwick, knows where they came from. (Chapter 7: Oriel.)
