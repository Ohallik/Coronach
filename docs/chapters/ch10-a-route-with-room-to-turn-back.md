# Chapter 10 - A Route With Room To Turn Back

Brief for every stream working on chapter 10. Plan: [SHIPPING_PLAN.md](../SHIPPING_PLAN.md). Story bible: [STORY_CAMPAIGN.md](../STORY_CAMPAIGN.md), movement V and the ending. How-to: [CHAPTER_GUIDE.md](../CHAPTER_GUIDE.md). Chapters 3-9 come first. **Chapters 9 and 10 are never cut.**

## What the chapter is for

One operation in five places: free Sorrel's nursery, break the blockade at Last Mooring, board the Continuance, stop Meret, open Stillwater's anchor, and bring the convoy and the young Choir through the last shared passage together. Nothing new is introduced. Every tool is one the player has already used (Cantor's collar, the sluices and couplings, Oriel's release, the Halo's joints), and every ally does a job the player watched them learn. The ending is not a choice on a menu: it is the convoy getting through, and then Sela turning back for a tug, because a route worth having has room to turn back.

- **What the party changes:** the clamps around the nursery are opened and Mother of Doors is freed. The Tollkeeper's cage is broken. Meret is beaten in three phases and stopped without being killed. The anchor is released by the allied crews. The convoy passes; a disabled tug is fetched; the rendezvous is made.
- **The ending:** a loose new neighbourhood under an unfamiliar sky. No return to the old order. Taren declines the permanent emergency-director job and goes to fix a handrail. Sela flies the next survey route and comes back. The fruit seller has moved her stall across Taren's safety line again.
- **Meret:** she is defeated as a formidable professional with a comprehensible objective. She orders her surviving crews to join the evacuation once her plan is lost; her help reduces the danger and supplies no missing solution. Civilian crews take her into custody, and the people she left out of the number are entitled to an account.

Sync in: about 27. Sync out: about 30. Play time: 90-120 minutes including the epilogue.

## Places

### SorrelSecondSky (space with ground shelves, combat)
The first moon, changed: the same ridge skyline, the drill mast, the crater, but Stillwater's **clamp** stands over it, the nursery's young are moving in the drilled voids, and an adult Choir, **Mother of Doors**, is coiled around the trapped nursery. Flight through apertures that were Hushwell's shafts; fixed ground shelves where a clamp's works must be opened on foot (docking transitions with checkpoints).

### LastMooring (space, combat) - rule: arenas divided by anchor chains
The approach to the last shared passage. Stillwater's anchor chains divide broad arenas; dockable maintenance islands sit between them; the convoy's hulls (every one a place the player knows) hold in the lee, visible the whole time. The **Tollkeeper**, an anchor-fleet carrier, closes the way.

### Continuance (dungeon, on foot, with flight between sections)
Meret's engineering vessel, carrying the last anchor: clean, well-kept, signed in a neat hand, and built by people who meant well. The boarding spine (Idra's crews brace it), maintenance decks, the anchor works. Compact crews hold it: some fight, some stand aside, a few help. The machinery is the game's earlier machinery, better made.

### StillwaterChamber (the final arenas)
Two dock-linked arenas: the open **frame gantry** on the Continuance's moving exterior, and the compact circular **control well** inside the anchor chamber. Three clear cover anchors survive every phase.

### ReturningPassage (space, the last flight)
The route opening: a fast run with the convoy through a living passage with the young Choir alongside, lanterns ahead, the herders' rhythm on the radio. Then a distress call from behind.

### NeighbourhoodAfloat (epilogue town, safe)
Familiar modules arranged into new streets under a different sky: the Halo's market district docked to Many Hands' arcade, the garden, the raft bay, a rib-arch from Lastlight over the square, Lanternwick lanterns on the gallery, the Crawl's sky-garden deck as a park, Hal's portable benches in three places. Everyone who came is here. A travel terminal for the route chart; the remaining side stories; the last scene.

## The fights

**Mother of Doors (Sorrel).** Not an enemy with a health bar to empty: a rescue expressed as a boss fight. The adult Choir wraps the nursery; four **clamps** pin her. Her retaliatory sweeps and pressure-waves are huge and readable, with generous lanes; she is frightened, not hostile, and the herders' rhythm on the radio narrows her sweeps as clamps come off. Open each clamp (fly its defences down, dock, perform the release on foot while the partner covers, launch). The fourth clamp is opened racing alongside her as she starts to move. She goes, and the young follow her out.

**Tollkeeper (Last Mooring).** An anchor-fleet carrier whose outriggers form a moving cage across the lane. Destroy chosen braces (break targets on the outriggers) to open gaps, weave out as the cage contracts, dock briefly on an arm to disable its gate motor on foot, launch before it retracts. Its guns fire marked lanes between the chains. When the gate motor dies the cage hangs open and the first convoy hulls come through behind the party.

**Keelbreaker is cut** (D223): the Continuance's defence is its crews and machinery, and the dungeon ends at Meret.

**Meret Venn and the Continuance (three phases, a checkpoint between each, no fourth health bar).**
1. *Ship to ship, in flight.* The Continuance itself: anchor clamps that reshape the lanes with visible warnings, tractor lines, a broadside of marked lanes, and Meret's own craft fighting from its shelter. Her shield returns fire the way a Flash Guard does: shoot it at the wrong moment and the shot comes back, so the lunge and the read matter. Disable her craft's three shield projectors.
2. *The frame gantry, on foot, on the moving exterior.* Meret in a heavy engineering frame using the ship's maintenance arms as weapons: a press (the Foundry Saint's slam), a welding line (the Works), a coolant flood (the Foundry's sluices). Machinery the player has beaten before, run by someone who understands it. Break the frame's two arm couplings.
3. *The control well, on foot.* Her compact emergency frame: a duel. Fast, precise, courteous. Her counter stance punishes mashing; her clamp-traps punish standing still; every attack is marked, and none of them is new. She talks during it, briefly, and listens. At zero she yields. She is not executed and does not fall off anything.

**The anchor** is then released by the crews: Tovra's gear on the clamps, Taren at the last collar because a lock is a lock, Idra's people bracing, Ishen reading the sequence. The player performs the final release (one hold, as at Oriel).

**The Returning Passage.** The convoy run. Then the tug *Welcome Back*, disabled and falling behind, and Bryn Asta telling traffic to go on without her. Sela turns back. Fly against the convoy's wake, clear the tow-drones off the tug, take its line, and bring it through as the passage narrows, with Sela's students leading the convoy by the lantern chain in the meantime. Demanding, forgiving: failure restarts the local section and never removes a town from the ending.

## Story beats (in order)

1. **Many Hands, among the Halo's districts.** One plan, said plainly, each crew naming its job. Meret's forty days are up.
2. **Sorrel.** The first skyline. Dace's outpost crew, still here, evacuating with their socks. Mother of Doors. The herders' rhythm.
3. **Last Mooring.** The convoy in the lee. A section hit; Driftreed's mats; it holds. The Tollkeeper. The gate opens.
4. **The Continuance.** Idra's crews on the spine. Compact crews who fight, who stand aside, who help. The captain of the *Steady Hand* lets them pass.
5. **Meret, ship to ship.** "I published the number. I never hid the number."
6. **Meret, on the gantry.** "You're very good at this. You were always going to be. That's not the same as being right."
7. **Meret, in the well.** The duel. She yields. Taren: "Tell your crews." She does: all Compact vessels to assist the evacuation.
8. **The anchor.** The crews. The last collar. The road moves.
9. **The passage.** The run. The young Choir alongside. Ilo reading names.
10. **The tug.** Sela turns back. Taren goes with her, obviously. The rendezvous, late, with the tug.
11. **Epilogue, some weeks on.** Neighbourhood Afloat. Shared passage rules being argued in the square (Ruva chairing, hating it). First messages from the worlds that stayed: Old Tern's, Aumi's, Senn's, Quill's. Meret, in custody, asked to account for the number by the people who weren't in it; she is answering. Orrin offers Taren the emergency directorship; he says no and goes to fix a handrail. Sela files a survey route with a return leg. Idra's cutter is tied up next to hers.
12. **The last scene.** Jun has moved her stall across Taren's safety line. "It did stop. Then the pears needed room." Credits over the convoy under its new sky.
