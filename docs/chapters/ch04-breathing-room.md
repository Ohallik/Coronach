# Chapter 4 - Breathing Room (Wendmire)

Brief for every stream working on chapter 4. Plan: [SHIPPING_PLAN.md](../SHIPPING_PLAN.md). Story bible: [STORY_CAMPAIGN.md](../STORY_CAMPAIGN.md). How-to: [CHAPTER_GUIDE.md](../CHAPTER_GUIDE.md). Chapter 3's brief and script (`docs/writing/Xylos.yarn`) come first in the story.

## What the chapter is for

The convoy now carries Tallow's cabins, Xylos's growers and three settlements that asked to follow, and its air is going stale: the scrubbers were built for one small station. Hadda, Rootmarket's mender, says the mats the seed house breathes through are woven at **Driftreed**, a raft village on the swamp world Wendmire. Driftreed's weavers won't send mats without weavers, and they won't send weavers anywhere while lights in the mire keep taking people. The lights are the lures of the **Rotbloom**, a predator that has made its home in a half-sunk Ancelane waystation and learned to copy Driftreed's lamps.

- **What the party changes:** the Rotbloom is killed (a genuine predator; this one is not misunderstood); the missing apprentice comes home; Driftreed joins as a floating district that keeps its rafts and its own say.
- **What it sets up:** reed air-mats that keep a breached section breathing in the finale; Maru Wend's tide-drifting district aboard Many Hands (chapter 5); the first working Ancelane machinery since Lanternwick (the waystation's pumps and sentries point at Hoarfell's Frozen Host and at Oriel); Driftreed is not on the Compact's list at all, because rafts don't count as moorings (Ilo's footnotes, chapter 5).
- **Taren and Sela:** Taren can't fix this with a repair; the thing has to be killed, and he doesn't enjoy how good he is at it. Sela, who navigates by fixed marks, has to learn a place where nothing stays put from someone who thinks fixed marks are a superstition.

Sync in: about 13. Sync out: about 15. Play time: 45-60 minutes.

## The people of Driftreed: the Ilu

Tall, slender wading people (about 2.1 m), long-necked and long-limbed. Smooth dusk-violet to grey-lilac skin, paler on the face and throat. A long gentle face with a short soft muzzle, large dark eyes. Two long **feathered antennae** rise from the brow and are the Ilu expression instrument (perked, drooped, curled, flattened back). A row of soft **glow-spots** runs along each brow ridge and cheekbone and down the neck: they brighten and dim with mood, and the Ilu speak across water in light as well as words. Driftreed's lamps pulse in the town's own rhythm (two short, one long), which is how you tell a real lamp from a lure. The Ilu dress in woven reed and oiled cloth, go barefoot, and are never far from a pole or a line. They have lived on rafts for longer than anyone remembers and find the idea of a foundation faintly sad.

## Places

### SporeVeil (space, combat) - rule: visibility is the resource
A bioluminescent spore nebula in soft greens and violets over Wendmire's three moons. Drifting **spore pods** burst into blinding clouds when shot or rammed. **Spore swarms** are drawn to light: firing, boosting and skills raise a glow gauge on the HUD, and a bright ship is mobbed (swarms cling and drain Thrust until shaken off with a roll). Flying dark for two seconds sheds them. Driftreed's **pulse beacons** mark the safe lane in three legs; one beacon is dead and must be relit under a Veil Ray's patrol. Maru is on the radio the whole way, rude about ship lights.

### Driftreed (town, safe)
Woven-reed rafts on rope bridges around a stilt market: the weaving hall (the landmark: tall drying racks of mats at the back), the smokehouse, Quill's pump raft, the floating school, the pilots' stage where the party's ships tie up. Arrival scene: the pilots are re-lashing a raft for the turning tide and Maru puts a line in Sela's hands. **The tide turns once** during the chapter (after the party accepts the job): two bridges swing away, two others meet, and the mire gate becomes reachable. The Compact's capacity notice isn't posted here; a courier pinned one up and nobody is on it.

### DrownedWaystation (wild approach, then dungeon)
**The mire approach** at dusk: mangrove trunks at the edges, peat islands and knee-deep channels (wading slows; marked water hides leeches), glow-fungus trails, and lamp-posts. Real lamps pulse two-short-one-long; lures flicker without a rhythm and are **Lurelights**. Then the **waystation**: a half-sunk Ancelane route station in the same carved stone and bronze as Lanternwick's relays. Three **pumps** drain it in stages: pump one drains the entry hall (Drowned Sentries wake as the water leaves them); pump two opens the lower gallery, where Pim is holed up in a dry pocket; pump three is at the door of the great hall. A fourth, optional pump (Quill's side story) opens a vault. Door checkpoint. **Rotbloom** in the flooded great hall.

## Map briefs

Same camera and rules as chapter 3 (fixed three-quarter view; tall things at the back and edges; every play-zoom still at or under 0.22 on `tools/look_density.py`; MAP_EYE_TEST).

**SporeVeil** (flight, about 1.5 km, 7-9 minutes). Three legs between four beacons, each leg with a different pressure: pods to thread (leg one), a swarm field where the party must coast dark between two bursts of boost (leg two), the dead beacon in a Veil Ray's circuit (leg three, relight it during the ray's far pass or fight the ray). Off-lane pockets hold two caches and the other two beacons of the side story. Driftreed's tide-gate, a ring of lamps on the water's surface seen from above, is the exit.

**Driftreed** (town, about 130 x 110 m, a first circuit of 4 minutes). Rafts are the districts; water between them is the floor's relief. The pilots' stage (south, arrival), the stilt market at the centre (Dov's smokehouse on its east side), the weaving hall north (back), Quill's raft west with its pump-and-press, the school raft east, the mire gate north-east (reachable after the tide turns). Twelve or more residents: weavers at the racks, children on the school raft, pilots coiling line, someone always fishing.

**DrownedWaystation** (about 380 m of route, 25-30 minutes). The approach runs south to north through three peat islands (a Bogstrider pocket, a lure ambush that teaches the rhythm, a leech channel with a dry detour). The station's broken portico is the landmark from the first island. Inside: entry hall (pump one), a ramp down to the lower gallery ringed by sentry alcoves (pump two; Pim's pocket off its west side; the vault off its east side behind pump four), a stair up to the great hall's door (pump three, checkpoint). The great hall is a round flooded arena about 36 m across with four lure-stalks on raised plinths at the rim.

**Rotbloom (boss, the great hall).** A floating bloom colony the size of a house, ringed by four **lure-stalks** on the plinths. Phase one, flooded: spore breath in a cone that leaves a slowing cloud, root lashes along marked lines under the water, a lure flash (a ring marking; turn it with a guard or roll through), a drag that pulls the party toward it. Burn a lure-stalk (a break target) and one of its feeding mouths gapes: the opening. With two stalks gone the last pump runs and the hall drains: phase two, the bloom grounded and thrashing, faster lashes that crack the floor, rot-pods seeding the rim. It is killed, and the lamps of Driftreed are the only lights in the mire again.

## Story beats (in order)

1. **The convoy.** Stale air; Abri counting heads; Hadda's tip remembered. Sela charts Wendmire.
2. **The Spore Veil.** Maru on the radio: "Whoever's flying the lantern, put it out." Three legs, the dead beacon.
3. **Driftreed arrival.** The line in Sela's hands. Ona Sedge: mats don't travel without weavers, and no weaver of hers is going anywhere while the lights are taking them. Pim, two nights ago.
4. **Around town.** Dov's gossip (three gone this season; the lights started after the old station's roof fell in); Quill and the old engineers; the school raft; the notice nobody is on. The tide turns.
5. **The mire.** Maru leads: two short, one long. The first lure.
6. **The waystation.** The pumps, the sentries (Sela: this is Lanternwick's stonework; Ishen would weep), Pim alive.
7. **The Rotbloom.** Killed. Taren, afterwards, is quiet; Maru notices and says nothing useful, kindly.
8. **Driftreed decides.** Ona: the weavers will go with their mats, on their own rafts, tied to nobody. Maru will come if she can design where they tie up: nothing fixed. Quill sends the pump drawings and stays.
9. **Chapter end.** Back to the convoy, where the lashed-together hulls have been given a name while the party was away: **Many Hands**. And a salvage crew has been asking after unmoored vessels. (Chapter 5.)
